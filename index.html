About Srithan Grand Palace Hall

Located in the heart of Kurnool, Srithan Grand Palace Hall has been the venue of choice for weddings, receptions, engagements and corporate events for over 15 years.

Our venues feature air-conditioned halls, royal stage decorations, in-house catering with veg & non-veg menus, ample car parking, guest rooms and full power backup.
